Hummagroove

Hum or sing a melody. Hear it back as a bass line and keys.

1. Hum your song

Hum each part of your song as its own section, like a verse and a chorus.

2. Arrange

Song order

    Record something to get started.

    Your melody Bass Keys Added instruments

    3. Add instruments

    Describe an instrument and what it should play. It will follow your melody's chords.

    4. Mix and play

    Set each instrument's level and position before you take the song into GarageBand.

    5. Export to GarageBand

    Sketch the song here, then mix it in GarageBand. The pack has a MIDI file with a track per part, a WAV of every track and your humming, and the full mix, all lined up at bar 1.
